01 / Our approach

Yoga should fit the person.

At Bhagath Yoga Classes, practice is an invitation—not a performance.

We’re a neighbourhood studio in HRBR Layout, Kalyan Nagar. Our approach is simple: clear guidance, thoughtful movement and room to listen to your own body.

Whether you’re new to yoga or finding your rhythm again, start with a conversation. We’ll help you ask the right questions before you arrive.

Good questions, answered

Can I join if I’m new to yoga?

You’re welcome to ask about getting started. Let the studio know it’s your first class so they can help you find a suitable option.

What should I wear?

Choose comfortable clothes that let you move freely. If you’re unsure about footwear or anything else, ask when you get in touch.

Should I bring a mat?

Mat and prop availability hasn’t been confirmed. Please check with the studio before your first visit.

How do I reserve a place?

Call or WhatsApp to ask about current availability. This website doesn’t take bookings or confirm places.

What if I have an injury or health concern?

Speak with a qualified healthcare professional first, then contact the studio and tell the instructor about any relevant movement considerations.

A few helpful companions

Props are here to support you.

Ask the studio what’s available to use before class. Equipment availability has not yet been confirmed.

01MatsA grounded surface for your practice.+

A yoga mat can provide a comfortable, steady surface. Check with the studio before leaving yours at home.

02BlocksA little support where you need it.+

Blocks can bring the floor closer and offer extra support in some postures. Ask whether they’re available.

03StrapsRoom to explore a stretch.+

A strap can help with reach while keeping a stretch comfortable. Ask the instructor how to use one safely.

04BolstersSoft support for slower moments.+

Bolsters may support restorative or seated postures. Availability at the studio is to be confirmed.
